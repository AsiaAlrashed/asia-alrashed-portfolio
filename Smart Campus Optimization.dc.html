Smart Campus Occupancy & Sustainability Optimization

A prediction model that does not stop at prediction: forecast occupancy, then let it drive waste collection, routing, transport, and emissions.

01 — Problem

A fixed schedule ignores whether anyone is there

Campus waste collection and internal transportation demand vary according to actual building occupancy. Static schedules therefore produce unnecessary trips, energy consumption, operational cost, and emissions, and they do it invisibly, because nothing in the process measures the gap.

Objective. Predict building occupancy and use those predictions to dynamically optimize waste collection and transportation operations.

04 — Optimization layer

Where prediction becomes a decision

Waste optimization

Predicted occupancy is converted into estimated waste generation. Containers at 50% capacity or above are considered for collection, so a truck is dispatched against demand rather than a calendar.

CVRP with OR-Tools

Collection is modelled as a Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem against a 3000 kg truck capacity, solved with OR-Tools, with NetworkX representing the campus road network and computing shortest paths.

Transport optimization

Transport demand is estimated from predicted occupancy, required trips are calculated against vehicle capacity, schedules are generated, and energy consumption and emissions follow from the resulting plan.
