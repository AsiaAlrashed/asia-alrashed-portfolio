03 · Engineering lifecycle

Knowledge versus conversational behavior

Reviewed factual validity and conversational behavior independently. A segment could support knowledge extraction, behavior training, both, or neither. Historical employee responses were not accepted automatically as correct examples.

Used structured classification outputs and manually reviewed reference examples to investigate disagreements. Reviewed classification quality with per-label precision, recall, and F1, keeping prompt-development examples separate from final testing.

Outdated facts, unsupported commitments, personal information, and exceptional arrangements were excluded or sent for review. Training splits operated at conversation level, with related customer conversations grouped where identity was available and duplicate checks applied before export.

04 · Engineering lifecycle

Completed model fine-tuning

Curated multi-turn conversational examples and completed fine-tuning of an OpenAI model for Arabic sales dialogue style and behavior.

Target behaviors included relevant follow-up questions, retaining known shipment information, understanding corrections, avoiding repeated questions, and avoiding invented prices, services, or actions. Reference responses were reviewed for correctness and conversational quality.

Changing service information remained in the external knowledge layer. Model adaptation addressed dialogue behavior, while approved knowledge supplied current operational facts.

05 · Engineering lifecycle

JSON knowledge graph & selective retrieval

Built a JSON knowledge graph representing countries, cities, routes, transport modes, services, goods, required documents, and load types. Relationships connect entities; scoped rules preserve conditions and exceptions rather than generalizing a special arrangement into a universal policy.

Knowledge comes from historical conversations, approved company policies, service documents, and operational clarifications. Customer statements describe a request; they do not establish company policy. Source references, review status, and version information support traceability.

The operating path retrieves relevant knowledge rather than passing the entire graph. Pinecone provides vector retrieval; the JSON structure represents entities, relationships, and rules. Pinecone is not presented as a graph database.

06 · Engineering lifecycle

Inference architecture & multimodal inputs

The conversation service combines customer state, approved knowledge, and media-derived context before generating a response or structured proposal.

Receive event and resolve identity
Load Odoo conversation state
Transcribe audio / inspect images
Retrieve scoped knowledge
Generate reply and structured proposals
Validate fields and actions
Execute permitted actions
Send response and record outcome

Voice transcription and image analysis are linked to the original message. Conversation state retains known customer and shipment details so the assistant can interpret short replies and avoid re-asking answered questions.

Runtime model configuration is versioned separately from fine-tuning experiments. The optional style-editor stage can be disabled and can remain off when a separate editing pass is unnecessary.

08 · Engineering lifecycle

Evaluation & regression testing

Evaluation spans data selection, retrieval, dialogue, and operational execution. Keeping these layers separate helps identify whether an error originated in knowledge selection, model behavior, state handling, or integration.

LayerChecks
Data classificationAgreement with reviewed examples; knowledge validity and behavior quality.
RetrievalExpected sources, correct scope, relevant exceptions, and exclusion of obsolete knowledge.
ConversationRelevant follow-up, corrections, repetition, unsupported claims, and handoff.
IntegrationValidated fields, CRM classification, confirmed actions, and duplicate prevention.

Real regression case: driver job versus shipment pickup

The bot correctly explained that driver jobs were unavailable but assigned an inappropriate follow-up classification. The fix grounded classification in accepted customer evidence and business rules. A paired test ensures that asking for a driver to collect a shipment is not rejected as a job request.

Production feedback becomes repeatable cases with expected outcomes. Available comparisons of prompts, knowledge, and code are not labeled as measured fine-tuning gains.

09 · Engineering lifecycle

Observability & per-conversation cost tracking

Odoo provides operational visibility, LangSmith traces model execution, and dedicated reports support quality and cost analysis.

Trace context

Model and configuration, prompt version, knowledge version, calls, usage, latency, supplied sources, validation and repair results, and errors.

Cost attribution

Message- and conversation-level aggregation across the main model, repairs, optional style editing, embeddings, audio, and image processing when used.

Usage-based costs remain estimates until reconciled with provider billing. Unknown usage is not silently counted as zero. Hosting, databases, Pinecone, and other infrastructure expenses are tracked separately from model calls.

Reviewed average latency, P95, call counts, and costly interaction paths. Areas of improvement include unnecessary editor calls, repeated questions, and repair attempts; changes are assessed through comparable before-and-after measurements.

A model trace is not proof that a message reached the customer or a CRM action succeeded. Monitoring distinguishes proposed, approved, and sent responses and confirmed operational outcomes.

10 · Engineering lifecycle

Deployment, reliability & security

The AI service is hosted on AWS App Runner using Docker images stored in Amazon ECR. Odoo runs in a separate server environment. GitHub manages source versions; deployment is associated with a specific release and compatibility checks.

Timeouts and bounded retries control external calls. Requests are retried only when safe; uncertain action or delivery outcomes are checked before repetition. Failures are recorded for review, and tracking-platform failure should not interrupt the conversation.

Secrets are kept outside Git. Permissions are limited by service and role, test and production environments are separated, and sensitive information is reduced in logs. Customer content and attachments are treated as data rather than privileged instructions.

Deployment and integrations were delivered collaboratively with developers.

11 · Engineering lifecycle

Weekly updates & continuous improvement

An operational weekly workflow processes new conversations and proposes knowledge additions or changes. Administrators receive proposals in Odoo with supporting context for approval, editing, rejection, or deferral before inclusion.

Approved knowledge updates maintain explicit scope, sources, and relationships. The workflow separates candidate information from approved operational knowledge so that a new employee message does not automatically become company policy.

Production conversations, human feedback, regression cases, retrieval checks, and cost reports guide ongoing improvements to data, knowledge, prompts, and application behavior.
