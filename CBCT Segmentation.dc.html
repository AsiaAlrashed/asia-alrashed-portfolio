01 — Problem

Voxel-level segmentation in a full 3D medical volume

The project focused on automatically segmenting the neural canal from 3D dental CBCT volumes. Unlike standard 2D image classification, the task requires voxel-level localization of a complex anatomical structure across a full three-dimensional scan.

Objective. Build an end-to-end pipeline capable of moving from CBCT volume to neural canal detection/localization to a 3D segmentation mask.

04 — Ground-truth mask creation

Annotation quality was part of the model pipeline

Neural canal structures were manually segmented and carefully refined using 3D Slicer. Considerable attention went into segmentation-mask quality because the model learns directly from these annotations.

Engineering principle: high-quality masks create cleaner supervision, which supports better segmentation learning.
CBCT volume + manual neural canal annotation↓ 3D Slicer↓ Mask refinement↓ Quality-controlled ground truth↓ Model training

11 — Engineering challenges

The work was more than running nnU-Net on a dataset

Large volumetric data

Representative scale: 12 CBCT samples ≈ 100 GB, requiring storage-intensive 3D data handling.

Ground-truth quality

Neural canal masks required careful manual creation and refinement, not just routine preprocessing.

3D computation

Training used 3D volumetric patches rather than conventional 2D images, increasing memory and compute demands.

Segmentation quality

The model needed precise voxel-level anatomical localization rather than a single image-level class.
