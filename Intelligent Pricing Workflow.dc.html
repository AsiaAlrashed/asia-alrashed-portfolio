Intelligent Pricing Workflow

Freight pricing moved out of chat threads and email inboxes into one tracked workflow, with rates read out of vendor emails by AI and delivered where the specialist already works.

( A ) The challenge

A pricing request had no home, so it could quietly disappear

Sales sent freight pricing requests to the Pricing team through WhatsApp. Because the request lived in a chat thread, it could be missed, forgotten, or arrive incomplete, and nobody could answer a simple question like which requests are still open, who is waiting, or how long this one has been sitting.

The second half of the problem was upstream. Rates arrive from GLA and from vendors as email, in different formats, so a specialist had to read through threads to find the numbers before they could quote anything. That reading time was the real cost, and it repeated on every request.

Both problems came out of sitting with the Sales and Pricing teams to understand the day-to-day process, not from looking for somewhere to apply a model.

No traceability

A request in a chat thread has no status, no owner, and no history that can be filtered or reported on.

Incomplete inputs

Free-text messages meant the details needed to quote often arrived partially, forcing a round trip.

Manual rate hunting

Vendor and GLA rates lived in email, so every quotation started with reading and transcribing.

Split context

The conversation about a request and the request itself were in two different places.

( C ) Architecture

One path from request to quotation

01Sales raises a structured pricing request
↓
02Pricing workflow: status, owner, filters, in-request conversation
↓
03Vendor and GLA emails attached to the request they belong to
↓
04AI extraction reads rates and key quotation details into structured fields
↓
05Pricing specialist reviews and confirms
↓
06Quotation back to Sales, with the whole trail attached
( D ) The solution

What I designed and built

Pricing Management module

An Odoo module that centralizes requests with statuses and filters, so open work is visible and nothing depends on someone remembering a message. The Sales and Pricing conversation happens inside the request, which keeps the discussion attached to its context.

Email integration

Relevant vendor and GLA emails are pulled into the pricing request they relate to, so the source of a rate sits next to the rate itself rather than in a separate inbox.

AI rate extraction

An LLM reads each email and returns rates and key quotation details as structured fields. The specialist gets a filled form to check instead of a thread to read, and the original email stays attached as evidence.

Vendor management

Vendors became records rather than names in a thread, with evaluation capability, so who is quoted and how they perform can be compared over time.

( E ) Technical decisions

Why it was built this way

Extraction, not generation

The model's job is to pull values that already exist in an email into fields, not to compose a quotation. That keeps the output checkable against the attached source and keeps pricing authority with the specialist.

Human in the loop

Extracted rates are presented for review rather than committed automatically. A wrong freight rate has direct commercial cost, so the workflow is designed to save reading time, not to remove the decision.

Inside the existing system

Built as an Odoo module rather than a separate tool. The teams already work in the CRM, and a request that lives beside the customer record inherits its context instead of duplicating it.

Structure the input first

Making the request a structured record was the prerequisite for everything else. Without a stable object to attach emails, statuses, and extracted values to, no amount of AI would have made the process traceable.

( F ) Impact

What changed in the process

Described qualitatively. This workflow has not been measured with a published metric, so no numbers are claimed here.

Requests became trackable. Status and filters replaced memory as the way to know what is open.

Less manual reading. Specialists receive structured rate information instead of searching email threads.

Context stayed together. The request, its conversation, its emails, and its extracted values live in one record.

Vendors became comparable. Vendor records and evaluation made sourcing decisions reviewable.
