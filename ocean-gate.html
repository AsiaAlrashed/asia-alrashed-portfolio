04 · Knowledge engineering

Retrieval and reviewed knowledge updates

Built RAG pipelines using LangChain, embeddings, vector retrieval, conversation history, and business context. Developed a JSON entity-and-relationship knowledge graph and indexed knowledge in Pinecone, retrieving relevant context for each request rather than sending the entire graph.

Extracted reusable knowledge from historical sales conversations, retaining scope, conditions, exceptions, and source-message references. Reviewed outdated information, conflicting claims, and customer-specific arrangements before treating content as reusable knowledge.

A weekly workflow processes new conversations and proposes knowledge updates. Administrators receive Odoo notifications and approve or reject proposals before inclusion in the knowledge base.
