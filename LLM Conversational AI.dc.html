Production Conversational AI — Fine-Tuning, RAG & Data Flywheel

A production Arabic customer-engagement system where fine-tuning shapes behavior, RAG supplies business knowledge, and Odoo turns interactions into reusable AI data.

The project combined historical WhatsApp and call-data extraction, conversational dataset preparation, LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning, RAG retrieval, omnichannel deployment, WhatsApp Coexistence, Odoo-centered conversation capture, and response-level feedback for future AI improvement.

( 01 ) Core engineering principle

Fine-tune for behavior. Retrieve for knowledge. Preserve data for improvement.

The system deliberately separates model adaptation from business knowledge. Company policies, services, and operational information live in the RAG layer. Fine-tuning adapts how the assistant communicates: tone, response style, Iraqi Arabic phrasing, and intent-oriented behavior.

Continuous Improvement

Fine-Tuning ≠ Knowledge Storage. Fine-tuning changes how the assistant communicates. RAG controls what business knowledge it retrieves and uses.
( 02 ) Business problem

Valuable customer intelligence lived as operational conversation, not AI-ready data

The company handled customer communication across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and calls. Those interactions contained real customer questions, sales patterns, customer terminology, recurring intents, Arabic usage, and Iraqi conversational style, but most of that material was fragmented across channels.

The objective was not simply to deploy a chatbot. It was to build a production conversational AI architecture while creating a long-term data foundation that could continuously improve future AI systems.

( 03 ) Historical data extraction

The dataset had to be built before the model could be adapted

Historical WhatsApp conversations and call data were extracted, processed, cleaned, and structured into conversational examples suitable for model adaptation. Raw operational messages cannot be treated as high-quality fine-tuning data without selecting useful examples, organizing conversational turns, and removing noisy interaction artifacts.

( 08 ) Data flywheel

Production use creates the next improvement dataset

The system was designed to preserve future interactions as reusable AI material. Automation and centralized channel integration make conversations more useful for analysis, evaluation, dataset expansion, future fine-tuning, and system improvement. This does not mean automatic retraining; it means the system captures the signals needed for future AI iterations.

( 09 ) Response feedback

Production mistakes become structured improvement signals

Added response-level negative feedback to flag incorrect bot answers, preserve them as improvement signals, and use this feedback to prevent known bad response patterns from being reinforced in future AI iterations. A dislike does not automatically retrain the model and does not guarantee a response can never appear again; it creates a usable signal for evaluation and improvement.
