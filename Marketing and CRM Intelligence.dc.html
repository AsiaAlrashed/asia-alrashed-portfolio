Part 1 · Omnichannel CRM · ( A ) The challenge

Customer history was scattered across three apps

Customers arrived through WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger, and each channel was its own inbox. There was no single place to see what a customer had already been told, who was handling them, or whether anyone had followed up. Whether a lead got a second touch depended on whoever happened to remember it.

The gap was not a missing feature, it was a missing workspace. Sales needed one surface where a conversation from any channel becomes a lead that can be assigned, progressed, and reported on.

Part 2 · Marketing attribution · ( A ) The challenge

Lead volume looked like performance, and it was not

Campaign reporting stopped at the number of leads generated. A campaign that produced many leads looked successful even if almost none of them qualified, and a smaller campaign that produced real business looked weak. Budget was being allocated on a metric that did not describe outcomes.

The missing link was between the ad source and what happened to the lead afterwards. That information existed, but in a different system from the one reporting on campaigns.

( C ) The solution

What I built

Channels into the CRM

WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger integrated so an inbound conversation becomes a CRM lead with its channel and source attached, rather than a message someone has to transcribe.

Workflow extensions

Business-specific features on top of the standard CRM for structured lead handling and follow-up, so the pipeline reflects how this sales floor actually works.

Attribution pipeline

Analytical pipelines that connect customer interactions and ad-source data with CRM outcomes, so each campaign can be read against qualified, won, and lost rather than lead count.

Reporting layer

Campaign quality surfaced as business reporting, which is what made the spend conversation possible with numbers instead of impressions.

( D ) Technical decisions

Why it was built this way

Capture source at intake

Attribution is only possible if the source is attached the moment the lead is created. Trying to reconstruct it later from timestamps or names is guesswork, so the integration writes it at the point of entry.

Extend, do not replace

The CRM was extended with business-specific workflow rather than rebuilt. That keeps the standard objects and their reporting intact and keeps the system upgradeable.

Outcome as the metric

Reporting was deliberately built on sales progression rather than volume. A metric that people optimize for should describe the result they actually want.
