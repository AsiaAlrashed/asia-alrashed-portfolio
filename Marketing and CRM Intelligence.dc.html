Marketing & CRM Intelligence

A Marketing Control Center inside Odoo connects Meta advertising performance with CRM outcomes, turning campaign spend, leads, funnel stages, conversions, reporting, and AI-assisted recommendations into one operational view.

01 · Business problem

Marketing spend and CRM outcomes were separated

Meta Ads Manager held spend, campaign, click, and lead performance. Odoo CRM held the actual lead journey and sales outcome. The team could see activity in one system and outcomes in another, but not the full path from spend to decision.

That made basic marketing questions difficult: how much was spent, which campaign generated a lead, what CRM stage it reached, which campaigns produced meaningful conversions, and which campaigns should be improved, paused, or given more budget.

Business flow
Marketing spend ↓ Lead acquisition ↓ CRM outcome ↓ Marketing decision
02 · Solution

What the control center combines

Meta advertising data

Spend, impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, leads, CPL, conversions, and ROAS pulled from the implemented Meta integration.

CRM attribution

Campaign and source context connected to Odoo CRM leads, stages, URL data, qualified outcomes, won opportunities, and lost opportunities.

Campaign control

Campaign hierarchy, performance views, create/manage flows, budget and targeting configuration, creative upload, copy, and pushing campaigns to Meta.

Reporting + AI Advisor

Saved reports, PDF/Excel/CSV exports, and an OpenAI-powered advisory layer for campaign optimization recommendations.

04 · Meta integration and campaign management

The implemented live connector is Meta

Synchronization

Meta API integration supports manual synchronization, automatic synchronization every 6 hours, and retrieval of current advertising data.

Campaign hierarchy

Campaigns, ad sets, and ads can be reviewed from Odoo with performance details connected back to CRM attribution and reporting.

Campaign control

The module supports activating or pausing campaigns, creating campaigns from Odoo, setting budget and targeting, adding creative and copy, and pushing campaigns to Meta.

Technical honesty

Google Ads, TikTok, and LinkedIn may appear as future platform options, but this case study only represents Meta as an implemented live advertising connector.

Campaign management loop
Odoo↓ Create / manage campaign↓ Meta↓ Campaign performance↓ Odoo analytics
05 · CRM attribution

The question moved from “how many leads?” to “what happened to those leads?”

Meta campaigns are connected with Odoo CRM leads through campaign, source, stage, URL, and attribution information. The important business view is campaign quality, not lead volume alone.

A high-volume campaign can be weak if the leads do not progress, while a lower-volume source can be valuable if it produces qualified or won opportunities. This is why the report follows the lead into the CRM funnel.

Attribution relationship
Meta campaign ↓ Leads ↓ CRM funnel ↓ Qualified / Won / Lost ↓ Campaign quality
06 · Analytics and reporting

Operational analytics without claiming unsupported connectors

Analytical areas

Campaigns, leads and conversions, website, social media, email/WhatsApp, and lead reports can be modeled and presented in the dashboard.

Connector scope

Meta and Odoo CRM are the primary operational integrations. Website, social, and email/WhatsApp automatic connectors are not represented here as implemented live connectors.

Report Builder

Reports cover leads by campaign, source, and CRM stage. They can be created, saved, edited, duplicated, exported to PDF, Excel, or CSV, and used to reduce manual reporting and app switching.

08 · Business value

What changed in decision-making

Described qualitatively. No numerical performance results are claimed for this system.

Spend became traceable. Advertising cost can be read alongside the CRM outcomes it created.

Lead quality became visible. Teams can evaluate funnel progression instead of lead volume alone.

Reporting became operational. Saved reports and exports reduce manual report preparation and app switching.

Optimization had context. The AI Advisor reads campaign performance as part of a larger attribution and funnel picture.
