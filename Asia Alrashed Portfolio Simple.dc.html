How I work

I am an AI engineer focused on building systems that move beyond experimentation and into real applications. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, generative AI, data, and business transformation.

Most of the difficulty is rarely in the model. It is in fragmented, imperfect business data, and in understanding a process well enough to know what should be automated. So I start with the process and the data, and I stay involved after training, through deployment and integration, until the system is actually being used.

Fine-tuning or RAG

I fine-tune when the model's behavior, tone, or language needs to change. I use RAG when the knowledge should stay grounded and updateable without retraining.

Data before modeling

Missing values, duplicates, inconsistent identifiers, and broken cross-source relationships get resolved first. A model trained on unreconciled data produces confident nonsense.

Systems, not notebooks

A model matters once it is serving something. That means APIs, containers, tracked experiments, and a real integration into the application people use.
