Financial News Sentiment & Stock Ranking

Designed and built an end-to-end financial NLP and data science pipeline that collects market news and stock data, structures and engineers the dataset, classifies financial sentiment using DistilBERT, and aggregates article-level signals into weighted stock-level sentiment scores for comparative ranking.

01 — Business / analytical problem

Convert financial news into a comparable decision-support signal

Financial markets generate large volumes of unstructured news. Reading individual headlines does not directly provide a structured signal that can be compared across stocks.

The project explored how news context, stock information, market response, and NLP sentiment could be connected to produce a stock-level sentiment ranking. The output is a comparative sentiment-ranking signal, not investment advice, trading performance, or a guaranteed recommendation.

02 — Data acquisition

The dataset had to be designed and collected

Financial news

Collected ticker-linked news records with headline, summary where available, publication timestamp, source, and URL where available.

Stock / market data

Retrieved stock price information around each news event so article timing could be connected to market response.

Confirmed sources

The notebook includes Alpha Vantage, Finnhub, yfinance / Yahoo Finance, and RSS-based Yahoo Finance feed collection logic.

04 — Feature & label engineering

Market response became an engineered feature

The notebook calculates price impact by comparing price before and after a news event. That transforms raw price observations into an interpretable market-impact feature used before the NLP modeling layer.

Implemented logic

08 — Pipeline orchestration and application layer

Beyond notebook-only analysis

Mage orchestration

Mage is presented as workflow orchestration: data collection, processing, feature engineering, sentiment analysis, ranking, and output. It is not the ML model.

FastAPI serving

Ranking results are exposed through an API layer so external applications or dashboards can consume the analysis output.

Why this matters

The hard part was not only selecting a transformer model; it was defining the data structure that connects unstructured news, temporal market data, ticker identity, sentiment, and a useful stock-level output.

NLP / ML

Engineering
