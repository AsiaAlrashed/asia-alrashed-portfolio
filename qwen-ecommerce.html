E-commerce conversational AI with Qwen & QLoRA

Developed a Qwen-based chatbot for e-commerce customer interactions and applied QLoRA fine-tuning to adapt the model to the use case.

ApproachEvaluation resultsRelated experience

Project scope

This project is separate from the Arabic shipping and sales assistant. Its model adaptation work uses Qwen and QLoRA.

QLoRA provides a parameter-efficient approach to adaptation through low-rank adapters and a quantized base model.
