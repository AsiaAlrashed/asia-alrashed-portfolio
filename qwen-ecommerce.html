WhatsApp E-commerce Assistant — Qwen 2.5 14B & QLoRA

Built a customer-support assistant connected to a dedicated e-commerce WhatsApp number. Fine-tuned Qwen 2.5 14B using QLoRA on data prepared from approximately 5,000 historical WhatsApp conversations between the e-commerce team and customers, while retrieving changing product, pricing, and local-delivery information from a separate knowledge base.

ApproachEvaluation resultsRelated experience

Business problem & project scope

The e-commerce team needed a WhatsApp assistant that could respond in a consistent customer-service style while answering questions about products, prices, and domestic delivery. These operational details change, so model adaptation and business knowledge were handled separately.

This is a separate project from the shipping and sales assistant. The Hugging Face Fanner2.0 experiments belong to that sales use case, not this e-commerce model.

Conversation data & model adaptation

Training-data source

Approximately 5,000 historical WhatsApp conversations between the e-commerce team and customers provided the source material for preparing conversational fine-tuning data. This figure refers to source conversations, rather than a claimed count of final training examples.

Qwen 2.5 14B + QLoRA

Applied QLoRA fine-tuning to adapt Qwen 2.5 14B to the team's conversational style and customer-support behavior. The adaptation focuses on how the assistant communicates and responds across a conversation.

Retrieval for changing business knowledge

The assistant retrieves relevant product information, prices, and local-delivery details from a separate knowledge base at response time. Fine-tuning establishes the desired conversational behavior; the knowledge base supplies the business information needed for the current question.

This separation allows business knowledge to be updated independently of the model's conversational adaptation.

WhatsApp integration & response workflow

Connected the assistant to the WhatsApp number dedicated to e-commerce customer support. The response workflow combines the customer's question and conversation context with relevant retrieved knowledge, then uses the adapted Qwen model to formulate the customer-facing response.

Offline adaptation

Historical WhatsApp conversations → conversational training data → QLoRA fine-tuning of Qwen 2.5 14B.

Customer interaction

WhatsApp inquiry → relevant knowledge retrieval → response using the adapted model → WhatsApp reply.
